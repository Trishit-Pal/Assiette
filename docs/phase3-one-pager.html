Assiette — an AI meal assistant for budget-constrained students in Paris

Trishit · ESSEC–CentraleSupélec DSBA M2 · Track 3 · One-pager for consultation with Mr. Weng Wei · September 2026 · Paste into Google Docs and share as “anyone with the link”

Phase 1 — The problem

International and low-budget students in Paris decide, almost daily, where they can eat a complete meal they can afford and are allowed to access. The decision clusters between 17:30 and 20:30: a remaining €1–€3.30, a metro ride, French signage, and a food distribution that may require prior booking.

What they do today is a patchwork. CROUS / CROUStillant show menus but do not reason over my arrondissement, budget, or arrival time. Linkee, Cop1, Restos du Cœur, Secours Populaire and others publish hours on separate sites, the Maison étudiante portal, and Instagram. Google Maps does not know student tariffs or eligibility. Group chats go stale. A wrong trip costs a closed university restaurant — or a volunteer asking for documents the student did not bring, with no French sentence ready.

I am the right person for a v1 because I live this as an international M2 student, I can test with classmates who will flag a bad recommendation immediately, and I can treat it as a data problem (live menus + a curated eligibility table) rather than a chatbot with opinions.

One sentence. No existing tool takes an English or French need (“13th, €3, dinner after 18:00”) and returns a grounded itinerary mixing social-tariff CROUS meals and free student distributions, plus the French to use at the door.

Phase 2 — Simplest AI application

Capability: retrieval + deterministic recommendation + grounded generation. The model is not allowed to invent venues.

Inputs. A sentence and optional filters (arrondissement, budget, meal slot, diet). Live Paris CROUS restaurants and menus from the CROUStillant API (region code 22; free; custom User-Agent; 200 req/min; non-commercial terms). A curated distributions.json (~15 Paris points: Linkee, Cop1, Restos du Cœur, Secours Populaire, Ordre de Malte, …) with hours, eligibility, coordinates, and last_verified. A small knowledge file of rules and door-level French phrases.

Two interactions. (A) Type a need → ranked 2–3 stop itinerary and a map of retrieved pins only. (B) Open a stop → eligibility, menu snippet or “bring card + bag,” dietary note, copyable French.

v1 will not build: live parcel inventory behind Linkee/Cop1 logins; voice; accounts; payments; native mobile; cities outside Paris; languages other than EN/FR.

Stack I can ship: Streamlit + Python + Groq openai/gpt-oss-20b (free tier). LLM parses intent, then must answer only from retrieved rows. Fallback snapshot if an API is down.

Biggest risk: a fluent answer that is stale or overconfident. Defence: timestamps on every card, a grounding contract, no claim of remaining stock.
